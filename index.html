About Our School

Sunrise Public School is a premier educational institution dedicated to providing quality education to students from Nursery to Class 12. Established in 1995, we have been shaping young minds with a perfect blend of academic excellence, moral values, and extracurricular activities.

Our state-of-the-art infrastructure, experienced faculty, and student-centric approach create an environment where every child can thrive and reach their full potential.
